          Where these measurements come from

          A trend needs readings taken on a timetable, not whenever somebody opens the app. A daily Windows task measures free space even with CleanDrive closed — it reads the drive and writes one line, and deletes nothing.

            Folders, by how fast they grow

            Each folder is compared only against earlier scans of that same folder. Scan one twice and it gets a rate; scan it once and it says so rather than guessing.

              Moved, and actually freed

                        Freeing the space

                        This is the only thing in the app that cannot be undone. It matches each item against the Recycle Bin’s own record of where it came from and when, so files you deleted yourself are never included — not even a file at the same path.
