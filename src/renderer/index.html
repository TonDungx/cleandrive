                Where these measurements come from

                A trend needs readings taken on a timetable, not whenever somebody opens the app. A daily Windows task measures free space even with CleanDrive closed — it reads the drive and writes one line, and deletes nothing.

                  Folders, by how fast they grow

                                  Language

                                  Applies immediately, to this window and to the notifications and confirmations the app shows outside it. Auto follows the display language Windows is set to — which is not the same setting as the date and number format.

                                  Appearance

                                  Auto follows the system, so a machine that switches at sunset switches this window with it. The same control sits at the foot of the sidebar.

                                  With a Windows contrast theme on, the app uses its colours — unless Custom is chosen: your own colours are drawn even then.

                                  Company while it scans

                                  A scan of a large folder is a wait with nothing to watch. A few of these walk the progress line while it runs, sit down, and now and then chase each other. They are drawn in code, they animate on the compositor, and they never touch the scan.

                                  Your own colours

                                  Pick eleven colours, or import a theme file someone made. Each change is checked against the contrast rules as you make it, and the colours cannot be used until all of them pass. While Custom is chosen they are drawn even over a Windows contrast theme.

                                    Keyboard

                                    Every control can be reached with Tab. Lists, the map of a folder and the photo grid move with the arrow keys, and Space ticks. Press ? anywhere outside a text field for the whole list.

                                    Scan history

                                    After every scan the app keeps a compressed outline of the folder — how big each folder in it is, and its largest files — so two scans can later be compared. It names files, so it never leaves this computer. The newest are kept, and one a month.

                                    Move to another drive

                                    For files you may want back but not on this drive. Each one is copied to a folder on another drive and the copy is checked before the original is touched. Put them back from Restore; the app never deletes them there.

                                      Off, the original goes to the Recycle Bin: nothing is lost, and nothing is freed until the bin is emptied. On, the space comes back at once — and the copy on the other drive is the only one left.
